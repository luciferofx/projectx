How It Works Control Flow

1
Install SriTu Hobby App
Download from Play Store and open the Bluetooth Car Remote.
2
Pair with HC-05
Power the rover and pair with "HC-05" (PIN: 1234 or 0000).
3
Drive with Joystick
The app sends 'U', 'D', 'L', 'R', 'S' commands which the Arduino executes.
