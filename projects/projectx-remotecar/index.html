Components & Wiring Hardware Map

Component List

  • Arduino Nano
  • Dual L298N Drivers
  • HC-05 Bluetooth
  • 4x DC Gear Motors

Wiring Map (Nano → Modules)

D2 (IN1) → Left Fwd
D3 (IN2) → Left Rev
D4 (IN3) → Right Fwd
D5 (IN4) → Right Rev
D0 / D1 → HC-05 UART

How It Works Control Flow

1
Install SriTu Hobby App
Download from Play Store and open the Bluetooth Car Remote.
2
Pair with HC-05
Power the rover and pair with "HC-05" (PIN: 1234 or 0000).
3
Drive with Joystick
The app sends 'U', 'D', 'L', 'R', 'S' commands which the Arduino executes.
