Wiring Map (Nano → Modules)

D2 (IN1) → Left Fwd
D3 (IN2) → Left Rev
D4 (IN3) → Right Fwd
D5 (IN4) → Right Rev
D0 / D1 → HC-05 UART

How It Works Control Flow

1
Connect via Bluetooth
Pair Android device with HC-05, open Serial terminal at 9600 baud.
2
Send ASCII Commands
Send 'U' (Forward), 'D' (Backward), 'L' (Left), 'R' (Right), 'S' (Stop).
3
Differential Steering
The Arduino toggles the L298N pins to drive left and right motors in opposite directions for zero-radius turns.
