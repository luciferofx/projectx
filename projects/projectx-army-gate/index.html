How It Works Step-by-Step Process

1
User Scans RFID Tag
MFRC522 emits 13.56MHz RF field and reads the card's UID.
2
UID Verification
Arduino compares the scanned UID against the authorizedUIDs list.
3
Access Granted (Match)
Green LED turns on, Servo rotates to 90°, barrier opens for 5 seconds.
4
Access Denied (No Match)
Red LED flashes, Buzzer beeps for 1 second, gate remains closed.
5
Auto-Reset
Gate closes automatically and system waits for the next card scan.
